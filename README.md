# 🚕 GibelaSA

### A community-driven public transport app for South Africa

GibelaSA is a cross-platform mobile application focused on making **South Africa's minibus taxi network easier to discover, understand, and navigate**.

The project starts with Johannesburg's taxi network, with the long-term goal of bringing taxis, buses, trains, BRT and other forms of public transport into one multimodal journey-planning experience.

> 🚧 **GibelaSA is currently under active development.**

---

## 📱 Vision

Public transport information in South Africa can be difficult to find, especially when it comes to the informal minibus taxi industry.

GibelaSA aims to build a reliable, community-driven source of information for:

- 🚕 Minibus taxi routes
- 📍 Taxi ranks and pickup points
- 🗺️ Route maps
- 💰 Typical taxi fares
- 📊 Route reliability and verification
- 📡 Community-contributed route data

Eventually, GibelaSA will expand beyond taxis to support:

- 🚌 Buses
- 🚆 Trains
- 🚇 Gautrain
- 🚌 BRT systems
- 🚶 Walking connections
- 🔄 Multimodal journeys

---

## ✨ Planned Features

### 🗺️ Explore Taxi Routes

Discover taxi routes around Johannesburg and view their paths on an interactive map.

### 📍 Nearby Routes

Use your current location to find taxi routes and ranks nearby.

### 🚕 Route Information

View information such as:

- Origin
- Destination
- Taxi rank
- Route map
- Typical fare
- Last verification
- Community confidence
